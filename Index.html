About Dreamora

DREAMORA is our school media club created for students interested in communication, broadcasting, journalism, sports commentary, radio, public speaking and creative media.

Your voice matters. Your ideas matter. Your story matters.

Our Categories

Five paths into the world of media. Choose where your voice belongs.

🎙️

Announcing & News

Present school events, announcements and news with confidence.

📰

News Editing

Create, organize and edit news content for the school media platform.

🏏

Sports Commentary

Bring sporting events to life with exciting live commentary.

📻

Radio Service

Explore radio presenting, interviews, audio production and broadcasting.

🎤

Public Speaking

Develop confidence and communication skills for speaking in front of an audience.
